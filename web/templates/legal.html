Privacy policy.

Effective date: September 12, 2026. Operator: Chief of Staff, a product of Hilbert AI Private Limited. Contact: hello@hilbertai.in.

What we collect

Account details you provide (name, email, password hash). Organization and membership records. Connected-account OAuth tokens (encrypted at rest), and the workspace content those connections expose: email metadata and message bodies, calendar events, Jira issues, Slack messages, and GitHub issues/PRs you authorize us to read. Preferences you write. A record of every action the agent proposes, approves, or executes.

How we use it

To sync your connected tools, identify actionable items, propose agent actions for your approval, and execute the actions you approve. To show your organization's activity history. To operate account authentication, billing, and support.

Google user data

Access to Gmail and Google Calendar data is used only to provide user-facing features directly in the app: reading messages/events to propose actions, and sending replies, marking messages read, or responding to invitations only after you approve. Chief of Staff's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to train general-purpose AI models.

Sharing with third parties

Live agent runs send source content and your preferences to the operator's configured AI model provider (Amazon Bedrock or Anthropic) solely to generate proposed actions. Connected provider APIs (Google, Atlassian/Jira, Slack, GitHub) receive only the specific approved action content (a reply, comment, status change, or RSVP) when you approve it, or routine actions explicitly permitted by your organization's automation policy. We do not sell personal data or share it with advertisers.

Data retention and deletion

Source data and activity history remain in your organization until an owner deletes them. Deleting an organization removes its source data and locally stored connection credentials. Deleting your account removes your personal memberships and sessions; it does not erase history a team you belonged to still owns. Disconnecting a provider locally does not revoke that provider's grant — revoke access in the provider's own account settings as well. Provider-side records and their own backups follow that provider's retention policy, outside our control.

Security

OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest. Passwords are stored as salted one-way hashes, never in plain text. Access to organization data is limited by role (owner, admin, member, viewer).

Your rights

You may request a copy of your account data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your account by emailing hello@hilbertai.in. Organization owners can delete organization-level data directly from Team & access settings.

Children

Chief of Staff is a workplace productivity tool not directed at children and is not knowingly used by anyone under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product changes. Material changes will update the effective date above.
